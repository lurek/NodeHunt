Wallet security begins with identifying what can go wrong: stolen recovery data, a malicious transaction, compromised device, lost backup, or social engineering.

## Protect recovery material

Never type a seed phrase into a website, form, chat, or browser extension. Store backups offline in a location appropriate to the value at risk. Test that you can understand your recovery process without exposing the phrase.

## Verify every transaction

Read the site domain, wallet prompt, network, destination, and requested approval. A signature can authorize meaningful actions. If a prompt is unclear, reject it and verify through official documentation.

## Reduce the blast radius

Use separate wallets for long-term storage, daily activity, and experiments. Keep software updated from verified sources. Regularly review and revoke unneeded token approvals.
