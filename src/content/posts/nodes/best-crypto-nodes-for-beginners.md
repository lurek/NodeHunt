The right first node depends on what you want to learn and maintain. A node is an operational commitment, not a passive-income category.

## Light clients

Light clients generally verify a limited part of a chain and rely on other peers for additional data. They are the lowest-barrier way to understand self-verification, but they make different trust assumptions from a full node.

## Full nodes

Full nodes independently validate more data and strengthen your personal ability to verify network rules. They need storage, bandwidth, updates, and monitoring. Start by reading official hardware guidance for the chain you choose.

## Validators

Validators add uptime, key-management, staking, and potential penalty responsibilities. They are not a sensible first step until you can operate a test environment reliably.
