<Callout type="warning" title="Not financial advice">
This guide is educational. A points balance is not money, and a roadmap is not a promise. Nothing here is investment, legal, or tax advice.
</Callout>

This guide is intentionally project-agnostic. New participation programs often combine a dashboard, an extension, a wallet connection, and a points system. Each is a separate security decision.

## Verify the official entry point

Start from an official website or documented social account and compare the domain carefully. Search ads and copied community posts are common phishing paths. Bookmark the verified page after your first visit.

## Separate your risk

Use a wallet with no meaningful holdings for experimental connections. Do not approve unlimited spending permissions. Review every signing request and revoke permissions you no longer need.

<Callout type="danger" title="Never share recovery material">
Never type a seed phrase into a website, form, chat, or browser extension. An account login never justifies access to recovery data.
</Callout>

## Document the trade-off

Write down the hardware, electricity, bandwidth, privacy, and time costs. This simple record makes it easier to decide when to stop.
