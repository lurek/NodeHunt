AI and blockchain overlap in a few practical areas: coordinating compute, recording provenance, managing incentives, and sharing resources. They do not automatically make each other more useful.

## Compute coordination

Distributed compute markets can help match hardware supply with workloads. The difficult work is reliability, confidentiality, latency, verification, and support—not issuing a token.

## Provenance and trust

An immutable record can show that a record was written at a time. It cannot prove the underlying input was accurate. Any provenance claim should explain who controls data entry and how disputes are resolved.

## Questions for readers

What user problem is solved? What trust assumption is removed? What information becomes public? What happens if the network incentives change? Clear answers distinguish infrastructure from a slogan.
