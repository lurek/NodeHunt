NodeHunt is an independent educational publication for Web3 infrastructure: nodes, DePIN, wallet security, and AI x blockchain.

## Our editorial standard

We aim to explain operating models, technical trade-offs, risks, and primary sources in plain language. We do not promise returns, treat points as money, or publish a project claim as fact without review.

## Corrections

Crypto infrastructure changes quickly. If you find an error, contact us with the relevant source. Material corrections will be recorded in the article update history.
